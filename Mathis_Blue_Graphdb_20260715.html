A =[N:1/+]>[S:2/-] B

Bonding Rules:
1. Through-Charge (N>S): N-Emission connects to S-Intake. Strongest bond (100%). Forms straight pillars.
2. Polar Repulsion: N>N or S>S is strictly 0% (charge collision).
3. Carousel Interlock (E/W/F/B): Requires opposite spins (+/-) to mechanically interlock without crashing. Max 65%.
